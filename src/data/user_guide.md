# BetterRoots User Guide & Knowledge Base

## 1. Introduction: The BetterRoots Philosophy
Welcome to **BetterRoots**. Our mission is simple: **Stop plants from dying in the first 28 days.**
Most plant failures happen because of "transplant shock" or improper stabilization. We don't just give you a generic calendar; we give you a **survival plan** adapted to your local weather.

---

## 2. Getting Started
### Your Garden Code
- **What is it?** A unique "Device ID" (e.g., `RSC5`) that identifies your garden without a login.
- **Syncing**: You can view your garden on another phone or laptop by clicking "Have a code?" on the dashboard and typing in your code.
- **Privacy**: Your garden lives on your device and our secure cloud, tied only to this code.

### Location & Weather
- We ask for your **Zip Code** to check your local **Hardiness Zone** and **Weather Forecast**.
- **Why?** If a Heat Wave is coming, we pause fertilizer. If it's freezing, we warn you not to plant.

## 3. Adding Plants (The Intake)
You have three ways to build your canopy:
1.  **Scan Receipt**: (Beta) Upload a photo of your nursery receipt. Our AI reads the text and finds your plants instantly.
2.  **Search**: Type a name (e.g., "Rosemary"). We'll suggest matches.
3.  **Manual Entry**: Didn't find it? Add a custom plant, and we'll assign a generic "Best Practice" schedule based on its name (e.g., "Fern").

## 4. The Care Schedule (Nurture Notes)
Your dashboard shows a 28-day countdown for each plant.
### The Tasks
- **Water**: Deep watering is key. Never "sip" (shallow sprinkle). Water until it drains.
- **Check**: Look for specific signs like wilting or spots.
- **Feed**: *Only* when the schedule says so. Fertilizing a stressed plant is like feeding a steak to a flu patient—it hurts them.

### "Care Paused" Mode
- If Sage detects **Extreme Weather** (e.g., Frost, Heat Dome), specific tasks will be **Disabled**.
- You will see a "Care Paused" banner. **Trust this.** Doing the right thing at the wrong time is fatal.
- **Triggers**: Temp ≤ 32°F (Freeze) or Temp ≥ 95°F (Heat). Sage checks all your garden locations automatically.

## 5. Daily Check-ins (Journaling)
Track your plant's health on its specific page.
### Statuses
- 🌿 **All Good**: Plant looks happy.
- 🍂 **Watch**: Something is off (minor yellowing, droop). Keep an eye on it.
- 🥀 **Act**: Immediate issue (pests, severe wilt, stem collapse). **Action Required.**

### The Journal
- Use the text field to note details: *"New leaf unfurling"* or *"Soil feels soggy."*
- **Why?** Sage reads these logs! If you report an issue, Sage can look back at your history to give better advice.

## 7. Troubleshooting Common Issues
### "It's Wilting!"
**The Moisture Test**: Before you water, stick your finger 2 inches into the soil.
- **Wet?** Do NOT water. Root rot looks like wilt.
- **Dry?** Water deeply.

### "Leaves are turning yellow"
- **Old leaves (bottom)?** Natural aging.
- **New leaves (top)?** Nutrient deficiency or root issue. Check-in with 🍂 **Watch** and ask Sage.

## 8. Panic vs. Patience: Interpreting Symptoms
Plants talk, but sometimes they mumble. Here is how to translate their language so you don't panic unnecessarily.

### ✅ Don't Panic (Patience Required)
*These are common "growing pains" during stabilization.*
*   **The "Sulky" Droop**: Slight drooping in the afternoon (especially Hydrangeas) that recovers by evening? **Normal.** Plants are just conserving water.
*   **Bottom Leaf Yellowing**: The oldest leaves turning yellow and falling off? **Normal.** It's shedding the old to make way for the new.
*   **Transplant Shock**: A pause in growth for week 1-2? **Normal.** It's building roots underground.

### 🚨 Raise the Alarm (Act Now)
*These are signs of serious failure. Log as "🥀 Act" immediately.*
*   **The "Crispy" Brown**: Leaves crumbling in your hand. (Severe Drought).
*   **The "Mushy" Stem**: Stems turning black, soft, or smelly at the base. (Root Rot / Overwatering).
*   **Sudden Collapse**: The entire plant falls over flat despite being watered. (Stem failure).
*   **Webs or Stickiness**: Fine webbing (Spider Mites) or sticky residue on leaves (Aphids/Scale).
